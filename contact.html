Consultation & Intake Protocols

Everything you need to know prior to your initial private diagnostic session.

What documentation should we assemble for the initial briefing?

▼

To derive maximum value from your initial consultation, we recommend having your most recent two years of filed returns (personal or entity), an entity organizational structure chart (cap table), and any recent correspondence received from taxing authorities. All items can be securely transmitted via our encrypted vault.

Do you sign client-provided Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

▼

Yes. While all consultations are strictly protected under statutory professional confidentiality codes and internal attorney-client protocols, we routinely execute customized family office and corporate NDAs prior to receiving any sensitive financial disclosures.

Can consultations take place in person at our family office or corporate headquarters?

▼

Certainly. For prospective clients on our Sovereign Enterprise tier or family offices managing dynastic transitions, senior partners frequently travel to private family offices across Geneva, London, Palm Beach, New York, and Dubai for comprehensive on-site strategy sessions.
