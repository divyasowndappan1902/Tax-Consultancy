Heritage & Leadership

Architects of Enduring Wealth & Global Compliance

For nearly three decades, Sterling & Co. has provided fiduciary certainty to the world's most influential families, founders, and institutional asset stewards.

Our Inception

Born from a Conviction: Tax is an Architecture, Not a Transaction

In 1998, former Big-4 international tax partners and senior counsel gathered in London and New York with a singular mandate: to establish a private practice free from bureaucratic conflicts of interest.

Traditional advisory firms prioritize high-volume compliance processing. Sterling & Co. was built as a private advisory studio where each client's wealth portfolio is treated as an interconnected financial ecosystem. We map statutory opportunities across multiple borders, safeguarding capital from erosion while maintaining pristine compliance records.

Today, our practice manages over $14.2 billion in client asset structures, providing partner-led representation across key financial hubs in North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.

Guiding Pillars

The Principles That Govern Our Practice

Four unwavering commitments that define every client engagement and legal opinion we author.

01

Absolute Discretion

Our client confidentiality protocols mirror Swiss banking secrecy standards. We deploy end-to-end encrypted vaults and strict partner-siloed communication.

02

Forensic Rigor

We do not rely on rough estimates or automated software assumptions. Every return, structure, and treaty position is backed by comprehensive legal research and audit defenses.

03

Proactive Foresight

We analyze legislative proposals months before they become law. Our clients' holdings are insulated well in advance of statutory shifts and budget announcements.

04

Uncompromising Ethics

We reject aggressive, non-defensible tax shelters. True fiscal mastery lies in leveraging established statutory incentives and multilateral treaties with total transparency.
