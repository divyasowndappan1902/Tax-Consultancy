Frequently Addressed Strategic Questions

Find immediate clarity on our onboarding protocol, retainer structure, and audit indemnification.

How does Sterling & Co. differ from the Big-4 accounting firms?

▼

Unlike Big-4 conglomerates where high-net-worth clients and mid-market firms are relegated to junior staff with billable-hour incentives, Sterling & Co. operates as a true boutique advisory studio. Every mandate is directly architected and signed off by a former Big-4 practice leader or tax attorney, ensuring nimble execution, personal access, and total discretion without corporate bureaucracy.

What is your protocol if our entity receives an audit notification from the IRS or HMRC?

▼

For all clients on our Growth Corporate and Sovereign tiers, audit representation is included at zero additional charge. Our controversy unit immediately issues a Power of Attorney (Form 2848), takes over all communication with the tax authorities, handles every document request, and represents you in conferences. You will never have to speak directly with an auditor.

How do you handle cross-border residency shifts (e.g., relocating to Dubai or Switzerland)?

▼

Relocating capital across borders requires an integrated approach. We coordinate the domestic exit tax requirements (such as IRS Section 877A or UK statutory residence test compliance) simultaneously with local establishment in Zurich, Dubai, or Singapore. We ensure double taxation treaties are invoked seamlessly so that income is never taxed twice during the transitional tax year.

What is required to initiate onboarding with Sterling & Co.?

▼

Onboarding begins with a confidential 45-minute discovery consultation under mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Following this briefing, our partners deliver a Preliminary Diagnostic Memo identifying immediate savings opportunities and exposure points, alongside a fixed-fee engagement proposal.

Can we engage Sterling & Co. for one-time transactional M&A or exit planning?

▼

Yes. While the majority of our clients maintain annual advisory retainers, we frequently represent founders and funds on dedicated special project mandates, including Section 1202 QSBS qualification, pre-liquidity trust funding, and sell-side transaction structuring.
